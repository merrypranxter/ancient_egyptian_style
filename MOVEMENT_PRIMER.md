# Ancient Egyptian Art Overview

## Visual System
Ancient Egyptian art serves as a visual language with its own grammar and syntax, communicating complex ideas through imagery.

## Periodization
- **Old Kingdom**: 2686–2181 BCE - Age of the Pyramids, emphasis on royal power.
- **Middle Kingdom**: 2055–1650 BCE - Focused on personal expression and narrative art.
- **New Kingdom**: 1550–1070 BCE - Expansion in both territory and artistic complexity.
- **Amarna Period**: A brief yet revolutionary phase focusing on naturalism.
- **Late Period**: 664–332 BCE, characterized by a return to traditional forms.
- **Ptolemaic Period**: 332–30 BCE, blending Greek and Egyptian styles.

## Core Invariants
- **Canon**: A strict visual system defining proportions and poses.
- **Registers**: Horizontal bands that organize scenes.
- **Composite View**: The portrayal of subjects from multiple perspectives.

## Remix Notes
- Consider variations in proportions to give new life while retaining core characteristics.
- Explore different materials and techniques that resonate with modern interpretations.
