# Ancient Egyptian Art Style Primer

## Understanding the Motif-First Approach
Ancient Egyptian art is deeply rooted in symbolism and the motif-first approach emphasizes the importance of motifs in expression.

## Key Characteristics 
- Use of hierarchical proportions and placement in accordance with religious significance.
- Flat perspectives and stylized representations.
- Symbolic use of color.

## Conclusion
This primer sets the foundation for exploring further elements such as canon, iconography, and practices that define Ancient Egyptian style.
