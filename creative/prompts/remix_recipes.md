# Remix Recipes — Ancient Egyptian Style

These recipes are creative fusion prompts that blend the Egyptian canon with other visual traditions or aesthetics. Each recipe is designed to keep the Egyptian visual DNA — flat forms, contour line, composite pose, symbolic color, register logic — while injecting a distinctive secondary style that produces something novel and coherent.

Each recipe includes:
- **Aesthetic Goal**: what the fusion is trying to achieve
- **Positive Prompt Addition**: what to add to a base Egyptian prompt
- **Negative Prompts to Keep**: what must stay excluded even in the remix
- **Full Example Prompt**: a complete, copy-paste-ready prompt

---

## Recipe 1 — "Neon Hieroglyphics"
*Cyberpunk meets Egyptian*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Retain the flat graphic logic of Egyptian wall painting — registers, contour lines, canonical poses — but replace the earth-tone palette with electric neon colors on a near-black ground, as if the tomb painting is a glowing display screen. The figures maintain perfect Egyptian form; only the light palette shifts into the future.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
neon color palette, electric blue and hot pink and acid green on near-black ground,
glowing contour lines, cyberpunk neon lighting (flat, not 3D), dark background,
chromatic color wash, luminous hieroglyphic text, LED-lit wall painting effect,
futuristic material on ancient form, synthwave color register
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
3D shading, gradients, photorealism, lens flare, volumetric light, fog,
western perspective, foreshortening, naturalistic anatomy, cyberpunk figure design
(no mechanical body parts, no implants, no western sci-fi aesthetic on the figures themselves)
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian wall painting style, canonical composite pose, flat fills,
bold contour outline — standing figure of Anubis, jackal head, holding was scepter and ankh,
striding pose, profile head, frontal torso, profile feet on groundline —
neon color palette on near-black ground, electric cobalt blue contour lines,
hot magenta and acid green flat fills, luminous glowing hieroglyphic columns flanking figure,
register dividers as neon lines, papyrus texture replaced with matte black surface —
no 3D shading, no gradients, no shadows, no perspective, no lens flare,
no cyberpunk mechanical parts, no western figure anatomy
```

## Recipe 2 — "Ink Sketch Canon"
*Minimalist line art*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Strip Egyptian painting to its essential skeleton — the canonical contour line, bare of all color. The result should feel like a master draftsman's preparatory sketch: confident, clean, unadorned, as if the senior scribe's grid drawing is the finished work. White or warm cream ground, single ink color, zero fill.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
minimalist ink line drawing, single black ink on cream or white ground,
no color fills, outline only, confident unbroken contour line,
technical illustration quality, preparatory sketch aesthetic,
negative space composition, Japanese sumi-e brush discipline but Egyptian subject,
clean white background, graphic design sensibility
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
color, flat color fills, gradients, shading, 3D, photorealism,
painterly texture, cross-hatching, stippling, scratchy line,
multiple ink colors, background elements, decorative fills
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian canonical figure proportions, composite profile pose,
18-square grid proportions, strict contour —
minimalist ink line drawing only, single continuous black line on white ground,
no color, no fill, outline only, clean confident line quality,
pharaoh striding figure, Khepresh crown, crook and flail,
cartouche shape in empty outline beside figure —
no shading, no color fills, no gradients, no hatching,
no background, no texture, pure line art
```

## Recipe 3 — "Gilded Icon"
*Byzantine / Egyptian fusion*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Merge two frontal, symbolic, hierarchical traditions — Byzantine icon painting and Egyptian formal portraiture. Both traditions depict transcendent beings in formal frontal or near-frontal poses with gold grounds, flat symbolic color, and a total rejection of naturalism. The result should feel like an icon from a lost faith that merged Cairo and Constantinople: gold leaf ground, formal pose, hieroglyphic inscription in place of Greek text, Egyptian divine attributes replacing halos.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
Byzantine icon gold leaf ground, gold background, icon painting style,
haloed figure (replace halo with sun disk or double plume crown),
formal frontal presentation, egg tempera texture (flat), jeweled regalia,
Orthodox icon border frame, rich lapis lazuli and vermillion palette,
gold outlines, sacred formal composition, manuscript border decorations
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
3D shading, perspective, foreshortening, photorealism, naturalistic anatomy,
Baroque, Renaissance, western academic painting, action pose, dynamic lighting
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian canonical proportions, formal frontal pose —
Isis enthroned, fully human form, throne crown, broad collar, outstretched arms with wings,
divine gold skin — Byzantine icon gold leaf background, rich gold ground,
formal frontal body with slight three-quarter face showing eye (blending frontal icon with Egyptian eye-frontal rule),
lapis lazuli blue garment with gold decorative bands, jeweled collar like Byzantine pectoral cross,
formal border frame with lotus and papyrus decorations instead of Greek text,
hieroglyphic inscription in golden columns flanking figure,
egg tempera flat paint quality, no gradients, no shading, no perspective,
no Renaissance anatomy, no Baroque lighting
```

## Recipe 4 — "Desert Surrealism"
*Dalí meets Egypt*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Keep the strict Egyptian visual syntax — flat figures, canonical poses, registers, symbolic objects — but subject the scene to dream-logic distortions. Clocks melt, scale ratios violate even the Egyptian hierarchical norm, objects float in wrong contexts, desert spaces open impossibly. The Egyptian figures remain perfectly canonical; the world around them behaves according to surrealist dream space. The figures are anchored; reality is not.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
surrealist dreamscape setting, impossible desert space, melting horizon,
floating architectural elements, inverted scale relationships (objects larger than buildings),
Dalí-esque stretched and warped background architecture,
hyperrealistic desert texture background (contrast against flat figures),
impossible shadow angles, multiple suns or moons, dream logic composition,
Salvador Dalí aesthetic in environment only, not figures
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
3D shading on the figures, foreshortening of figures, distorted figure anatomy
(the figures must remain canonically correct — only environment is surreal),
photorealistic figures, naturalistic figure anatomy
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian wall painting style, canonical composite pose, flat color fills,
bold black contour outline — Osiris standing mummiform, Atef crown,
crook and flail, green skin, perfectly canonical pose, flat color, contour line —
placed in surrealist impossible desert dreamscape, melting sandstone obelisks,
two suns hanging in purple-ochre sky, hyper-detailed photorealistic desert sand
contrasting with flat canonical figure, floating judgment scales suspended in air,
scale of the Ammit creature larger than the obelisks, dream-logic spatial ruptures,
Dalí stretched architectural shadows, impossible depth — canonical figures remain flat
and iconic against surrealist environment — no 3D shading on figures,
no foreshortening of figures, no distorted figure anatomy
```

## Recipe 5 — "Papyrus Noir"
*Noir / shadow + Egyptian flatness*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Maintain the flat graphic quality of Egyptian art while importing the high-contrast black-and-white chiaroscuro palette and narrative mood of film noir. Not 3D shading, but stark 1-bit or 2-bit tonal contrast: figures are pure black silhouettes or pure white outlines on a darkly toned aged paper ground. Hieroglyphs act as noir caption text. The mood is ominous, the space is flat, but the visual drama of hard light/dark contrast is maximized within the flat medium.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
high contrast black and white palette only, stark silhouette and line,
aged sepia or dark amber ground, noir graphic novel visual language,
1950s crime pulp cover aesthetic (but Egyptian subject),
heavy black areas, strong light/dark opposition, flat graphic noir,
expressionistic shadow shapes as flat graphic forms (not 3D shadows),
hieroglyphs as caption text, dramatic composition within register logic
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
color, 3D rendered shadows (shadows are flat graphic shapes only, not volumetric),
photorealism, gradients, soft blending, anime, fantasy, contemporary illustration default
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian canonical proportions, composite profile pose,
register composition, flat graphic quality — Anubis leaning over a mummy on a bier,
judgment scales in background — stark black and white only,
noir graphic novel palette, aged dark amber papyrus ground,
figure in heavy black silhouette with white contour line detail,
dramatic flat black shadow shape behind figure (graphic, not volumetric),
hieroglyphic panel above acting as caption, border of tyet knots and wedjat eyes,
1950s noir graphic sensibility on Egyptian form —
no color fills, no 3D shading, no gradients, no photorealism,
no volumetric light, no lens effects
```

## Recipe 6 — "Chromatic Override"
*Keep canon, swap palette to unexpected colors*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Preserve every canonical rule — composite pose, flat fill, register composition, bold contour, hierarchical scale, correct iconography — but replace the standard Egyptian palette (earth tones, Egyptian blue, black, white, gold) with a radically different but internally consistent color scheme. Suggested overrides: monochrome violet, acid yellow on indigo, forest green palette, rose-gold duotone. The form is perfectly Egyptian; the color is alien to it.

**Positive Prompt Addition (violet override example):**
```
monochrome violet and lavender palette only, all colors replaced with violet spectrum,
lavender fills, deep purple outlines, pale violet ground, violet hierarchy of tones
(darkest for outlines, mid-violet for flat fills, pale lavender for ground),
no earth tones, no Egyptian traditional colors, chromatic palette substitution
```

**Positive Prompt Addition (acid yellow on indigo example):**
```
deep indigo ground, acid yellow outlines and fills, high contrast two-color palette,
electric yellow contour lines on dark indigo, minimal flat fills,
silkscreen print aesthetic, bold graphic design palette
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
3D shading, gradients, shadows, perspective, foreshortening,
naturalistic color, photorealistic color, traditional Egyptian earth tones
(for the override effect), standard Egyptian blue and ochre palette
```

**Full Example Prompt (violet monochrome):**
```
ancient Egyptian wall painting style, canonical composite pose, flat fills,
bold contour outline, register composition, 18-square proportions —
Horus standing striding figure, falcon head, double crown, was scepter —
monochrome violet and lavender palette only, deep violet contour lines,
mid-violet flat fills, pale lavender ground, no earth tones, no gold,
no traditional Egyptian colors, internally consistent violet tonal hierarchy,
flat Egyptian graphic quality in violet spectrum —
no 3D shading, no gradients, no perspective, no shadows,
no naturalistic color, no photorealism
```

## Recipe 7 — "Monumental Mosaic"
*Tile / mosaic texture on Egyptian forms*

**Aesthetic Goal:** Retain the flat, symbolic, register-organized logic of Egyptian art but render its surface as if composed of ceramic or glass mosaic tiles — each color region broken into visible tesserae, the outlines formed by dark grout lines rather than painted contour. The result evokes both the mosaic traditions of Ravenna and the flat, programmatic iconic tradition of Egypt. The tesserae should be visible but not disruptive to the legibility of the canonical figure.

**Positive Prompt Addition:**
```
mosaic tile texture, visible ceramic tesserae, grout lines between color regions,
Byzantine mosaic technique applied to Egyptian subject,
irregular but organized tile grid texture within each flat color region,
stone or glass tile material, small square tile units,
Ravenna-style mosaic execution, gold tesserae for divine elements,
matte ceramic surface, slightly uneven tile placement for handcrafted quality
```

**Negative Prompts to Keep:**
```
3D shading, perspective, photorealistic rendering, painted surface (suppress brushstroke),
smooth gradients (the tile texture replaces all smooth color transitions),
naturalistic anatomy, cartoon smoothness, digital art default
```

**Full Example Prompt:**
```
ancient Egyptian canonical figure, composite profile pose, flat register composition,
hierarchical scale — pharaoh in smiting pose, Khepresh crown, raising mace,
diminutive enemies at feet — rendered as Byzantine-style ceramic mosaic,
visible square tesserae grid within each color region, dark grout outline lines
replacing painted contour line, gold glass tesserae for the crown and regalia,
turquoise ceramic tiles for kilt area, deep red tiles for background register bands,
black grout line contour on all figures, Ravenna mosaic execution quality,
matte ceramic surface, slight irregularity in tile placement —
no 3D shading, no gradients, no smooth paint surface, no photorealism,
no perspective depth, no western anatomy
```
